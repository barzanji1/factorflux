---
name: Client carrier access and funding
description: User-approved carrier verification and the boundary between tracking and actual payment.
---

Carrier access requires verified ownership of an existing carrier contact email. A unique matching email activates automatically; no match or an ambiguous match requires admin approval. A claimed company name or public MC number never authorizes another carrier's data.

**Why:** The user explicitly chose verified contact-email activation and admin approval for unmatched registrations.

**How to apply:** Keep client credentials separate from staff and broker authentication, and preserve carrier ownership boundaries when accounts are reviewed or reassigned.

“Mark Funded” reflects funding already verified/completed in the existing financial workflow. It must not initiate payment. Reverting a tracking step must not reverse the financial transaction.

**Why:** The user explicitly said the button “reflects status, not triggers payment.”

**How to apply:** Preserve the existing financial approval, payout and settlement workflow; never use a manually edited submission status as evidence of actual payment.
