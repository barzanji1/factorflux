---
name: Contractor payroll policy
description: 1099 statement terminology, visibility, financial rules, and correction requirements.
---
Agents are 1099 contractors: approved hours × hourly rate equals both gross and net pay. Do not add withholding, FICA, deduction lines, or employee overtime assumptions. Use “Pay Statement,” the company heading “Trucking Pros LLC,” and the note “1099 contractor — no taxes withheld.”

Only admins run payroll or change rates. Contractors can see their own hourly rate on their own issued statements, but not rate settings/history or anyone else's statements.

Locked time requires an explicit audited unlock before correction. Issued statements are immutable; corrections void the prior statement and issue a new one. Preserve original PDFs and rate snapshots.

**Why:** The user specified a contractor-only workflow and explicitly clarified that contractors should see the rate on their own statements.

**How to apply:** Preserve these boundaries in payroll, PDFs, access rules, and future annual reporting. Annual gross must remain queryable, but 1099-NEC generation was explicitly excluded.
