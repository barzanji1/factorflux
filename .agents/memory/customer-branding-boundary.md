---
name: Customer branding boundary
description: TPF branding belongs on all customer-facing outputs; FactorFlux remains inside the staff app.
---

All customer-facing outputs use Trucking Pros Financial branding: emails, funding receipts, carrier portal, broker confirmation/dispute pages, NOA/release letters, and printable/exportable documents. FactorFlux branding stays only inside the internal app (sidebar, login, admin pages).

**Why:** The user explicitly separated the customer-facing company brand from the internal application brand.

**How to apply:** Use the supplied TPF mark or the company wordmark on customer surfaces. Keep the portal URL factorflux.io unchanged. Do not rename infrastructure identifiers or internal app assets merely because their names contain FactorFlux.
