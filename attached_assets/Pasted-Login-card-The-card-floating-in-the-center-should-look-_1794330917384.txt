Login card: The card floating in the center should look like a real piece of glass sitting in front of the building. This means you should be able to SEE the building through the card — blurred and frosted, but visible. The card is NOT a solid color. It is NOT gray. It is NOT opaque. It is transparent white at very low opacity — think looking through a frosted shower door or a frosted window. The background bleeds through. The only thing making the card visible is a very subtle white tint, a strong backdrop blur that frosts whatever is behind it, and a thin bright white border around the edge like the edge of real glass catching light. There should be a subtle white gradient fading from the top of the card downward to simulate light hitting the top of the glass. One thin bright white line across the very top edge of the card as a light reflection. That is it. No heavy shadows. No gray backgrounds. No solid colors. The card should feel like it is floating and made of ice or crystal.

Inputs: The email and password fields inside the card should also be glass — slightly more frosted than the card itself but still transparent. Not gray boxes. Not solid backgrounds. Glass inside glass. White text, white placeholder text at half opacity, thin white borders.

Sign In button: Teal gradient matching the TPF brand color. This is the only solid-colored element on the entire page. It should pop against all the glass.

Logo and text: The FactorFlux logo, “Freight Factoring OS” subtitle, and “Trucking Pros Financial” name are all white with a very subtle text shadow for readability against the glass.

Key technical requirement: The backdrop-filter blur and saturate properties are what create the liquid glass illusion. The card background must be nearly transparent white so the backdrop-filter can do its work. If the background is opaque, backdrop-filter has nothing to show through and the glass effect is killed. That is exactly what has been going wrong — the background keeps getting set too opaque which turns the glass into a solid gray wall.
