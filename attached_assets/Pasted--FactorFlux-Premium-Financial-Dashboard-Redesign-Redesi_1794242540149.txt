# FactorFlux — Premium Financial Dashboard Redesign

Redesign the **Agent Dashboard's visual appearance only** to match the sophisticated institutional finance aesthetic established by our new login page.

**CRITICAL: This is a visual reskin, NOT a functional or structural redesign.**

### Design language

- Sidebar: deep institutional navy `#102A38`
- Workspace background: refined off-white `#F5F7F8`
- Card surfaces: porcelain white `#FFFFFF`
- Primary accent: muted financial teal `#087F87`
- Primary text: charcoal `#263D40`
- Secondary text: slate `#72878A`
- Borders: subtle cool-gray `#DFE5E8`
- Shadows: soft, restrained, and realistic

Replace the current washed-out gray neumorphism with premium financial UI styling. Use subtle glass highlights and dimensional depth selectively, not excessive transparency or neon effects.

### Preserve the existing dashboard EXACTLY

- Work at a Glance horizontal KPI carousel, including sliding behavior and navigation arrows.
- All six floating quick-action cards, their current positions, dimensions, and arrangement.
- All six card flip animations and front/back interactions.
- Financial Overview charts and existing data visualizations.
- Sidebar navigation, grouping, and expand/collapse behavior.
- Top toolbar, search, notifications, clock-in, profile, and New menu.
- All existing labels, metrics, content, and functionality.

Do not restructure, reposition, resize, remove, or replace these components.
