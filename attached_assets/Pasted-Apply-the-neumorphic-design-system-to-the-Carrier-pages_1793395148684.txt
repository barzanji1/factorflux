CARRIER LIST (/carriers or wherever the list lives):

CARRIER PROFILE/DETAIL:

- Tab strip (whatever tabs exist — Overview, Invoices, Documents, etc.): NeuTabs.

CHARTS & VISUALIZATIONS (this is where the numbers get visual):
- "Invoice Volume" — 3D cylinder bar chart showing invoices per month for this carrier. Same Recharts custom bar shape as the dashboard (rounded cap, gradient sides, teal color). NeuCard concave background.
- "Revenue by Load Type" — donut or pie chart showing $ breakdown by OTR/Dump/Hotshot/Other. Teal/cyan/gray palette. Center text shows total $. NeuCard concave.

- Layout: charts in a 2x2 grid below the header, above the tabs. Each chart card has a title and a small "View in Reports →" link.
